I'm an Email Security Engineer with 5+ years of information security experience, including 2+ years focused on email security engineering and operations. I specialize in architecting and operating advanced email protections using Proofpoint (TAP, TRAP, DLP) and Microsoft Defender for Office 365 (EOP, Safe Links, Safe Attachments, KQL threat hunting).

I have deep proficiency in Microsoft Purview DLP — designing classification label taxonomies, tuning EDM and regex-based detectors, managing exception governance, and running split tests and drift monitoring at enterprise scale. I harden email trust through SPF/DKIM/DMARC design and enforcement, DMARC reporting analysis, display name and VIP impersonation controls, QR code phishing detection, and BEC pattern defense.

I build API/SOAR-integrated automation playbooks using PowerShell and Python, define program metrics (catch rate, FP rate, MTTD/MTTR, DLP signal quality), and translate complex email threat findings into crisp, actionable insights for engineers, business stakeholders, and senior leadership.

Currently completing my M.S. in Cybersecurity at Wright State University (2024–2025), with coursework in Email & Messaging Security, IAM, Cloud Security, and DLP.
